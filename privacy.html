Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Osaka Dining Compass, developed by Kentaro Iwata, handles information when you use the application.

1. Information Collected by the Developer

Osaka Dining Compass does not require account registration. The developer does not collect names, email addresses, telephone numbers, payment information, or other personally identifiable information through the app.

The app does not contain advertising, user tracking, third-party advertising SDKs, or VPN functionality.

2. Location Information

Some features may request permission to access your location, such as displaying your current position, finding nearby destinations, or assisting with navigation.

The developer does not collect or store your precise location. Location information is used only when necessary to provide the feature you choose to use. Location information may also be processed by your device, mapping provider, or navigation service according to that provider's privacy policy.

You can disable location access at any time in your device's Settings application.

3. Analytics and Advertising

Osaka Dining Compass does not use advertising networks and does not use personal information for targeted advertising. The developer does not create advertising profiles or track users across apps or websites.

4. External Websites and Services

The app may include links to external services, including map services, transportation services, restaurant websites, reservation services, and other third-party websites.

When you open an external website or service, that service may collect or process information according to its own privacy policy. The developer is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of third-party services.

5. Data Sharing

Because the developer does not collect personal information through the app, the developer does not sell, rent, or share personal information with third parties.

6. Children's Privacy

Osaka Dining Compass is not designed to collect personal information from children. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

The developer takes reasonable measures to design the app in a manner that minimizes the collection and storage of personal information. However, no electronic system or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's functions, applicable laws, or data-handling practices change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
