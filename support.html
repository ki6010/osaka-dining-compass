Help, feedback, and technical support

Contact Support

For questions, technical problems, incorrect information, or suggestions concerning Osaka Dining Compass, please contact the developer by email.

Contact Support

Email: diningtable.project@gmail.com

Developer: Kentaro Iwata

When Contacting Support

To help identify the issue, please include the following information when possible:

  • Your iPhone model
  • Your iOS version
  • The app version
  • A description of the problem
  • The steps that caused the problem
  • A screenshot, if appropriate

Location Services

Some map and nearby-location features may require permission to access your location.

You can review or change this permission by opening:

Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Osaka Dining Compass

External Links

Restaurant, map, reservation, transportation, and other external links may open third-party websites or applications. Availability and content are managed by the respective third-party service.

Incorrect or Outdated Information

Restaurant hours, prices, menus, reservation availability, transportation information, and sightseeing information may change. Please contact support if you notice information that should be corrected.
